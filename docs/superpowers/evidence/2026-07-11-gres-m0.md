## Checked pooler deviations

PgDog 0.1.6 does not track SET issued after BEGIN, returns raw `17` rather than
canonical `17ms` from its logical SHOW map, and forwards RESET without updating
that map. The live test restores tracked startup state before backend release,
proves startup-state replay only, keeps RESET and observation in one checkout,
and does not claim SET-change or post-RESET replay. These pinned-pooler limits
are recorded in `crates/gres-conformance/pooler-baseline.md`; they are not Gres
deviations from PostgreSQL 18.
